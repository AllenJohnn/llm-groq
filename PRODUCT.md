# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers and independent model users who run LLM ShardX in browsers across phones, laptops, and desktops. They create or join a room, contribute device memory and compute, select a local model, and follow a shared answer.

## Product Purpose

LLM ShardX coordinates browser devices to run language models locally across their GPUs. Success means users can understand room membership, model readiness, and answer progress at a glance, then ask a question with confidence that prompts stay within their room.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, premium, precise. The experience should feel considered and native on Apple devices while remaining at home in other modern browsers.

## Anti-references

Avoid busy layouts, saturated decoration, gratuitous motion, and interfaces that resemble a generic analytics dashboard. Glass should clarify hierarchy and depth, not obscure content.

## Design Principles

- Keep the active task and conversation central.
- Make room, model, and device status easy to scan.
- Use restrained surfaces and clear contrast to support long sessions.
- Let motion explain state changes and honor reduced-motion preferences.
- Preserve the same calm identity across the product and its landing page.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, responsive touch targets, and respect for `prefers-reduced-motion`.
