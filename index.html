WHAT IS WEBSLICE

WebGPU-accelerated Sharded LLM Inference
across Compute-constrained Edge devices.

Big models like Qwen 3.8 are too large for any single phone or laptop. That is why AI lives in data centers.

WebSLICE splits the model across every device in a room: each one downloads just its slice, and every word of the answer takes a lightning lap through all of them before it appears on every screen at once.

No install. No accounts. No servers. Your words never leave the room.
